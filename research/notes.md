# Research Notes: Verified New Jersey Food Trucks

**Status:** complete
**Depth:** Quick

## Plan

- **Question:** Which currently operating New Jersey food trucks can be added to the directory with enough first-party evidence to mark them verified?
- **Scope:** New Jersey-based or New Jersey-operating food trucks with an official website or official social profile, excluding unsupported schedules, locations, images, and claims.
- **Audience:** South Jersey Food Truck directory visitors and event planners.
- **Deliverable:** A small set of evidence-backed JSON listings with source URLs and verification dates.

- Marketplace or statewide booking services without an identifiable individual truck were excluded.

## Conflicts & Open Questions

The official sources are promotional Tier 3 evidence, but they are first-party and sufficient for operator identity, mobile-food activity, New Jersey location/service, and direct contact details. Schedules and hours remain intentionally unverified.

## Gaps

Candidates without a specific New Jersey base or identifiable operator profile were excluded rather than filled with assumptions. Operators with a documented primary New Jersey service city were included without describing that city as a legal headquarters.
