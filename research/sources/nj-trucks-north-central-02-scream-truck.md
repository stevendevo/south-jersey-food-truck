- ### What is Scream Truck?



























Scream Truck is a modern ice cream truck experience for neighborhood routes, private parties, and community events throughout select towns in New Jersey. Our trucks feature real, feel-good pop music, high quality ingredients, customizable LED screens, and a simple online booking process.

- ### How do Reserved Routes work?



























It's simple! We'll text you when we're coming to your neighborhood. Simply Reply Y to reserve a stop, place your order, and Scream Truck will deliver within your one-hour window.

- ### How do I book Scream Truck for an event?



























You can book New Jersey events online in just a few minutes. Enter your address and we'll let you know which event packages are available. Pick a time, give us some info about your guest of honor, and we'll do the rest!

- ### What areas do you serve?



























We serve select neighborhoods in New Jersey across Union, Essex, Somerset, Middlesex, Bergen, Passaic, Morris, and Monmouth Counties.

- ### What types of events are best for Scream Truck?



























Scream Truck is perfect for birthdays, school events, corporate events, neighborhood block parties, mitzvahs, weddings, and community events around New Jersey.

- ### How far in advance should I book?



























We recommend booking at least 30-60 days in advance, and earlier in New Jersey peak season (May-Sep). Popular dates and weekends fill up quickly!
