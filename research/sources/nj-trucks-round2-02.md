## Jersey Rollz is a fun and inventive food truck

We’re serving up gourmet egg rolls with a twist.  We have taken a unique and flavorful approach to the all too familiar burger, sandwich, or wrap.  This unique spin on the traditional egg roll combined with our homemade gourmet sauces will leave you with an unforgettable food truck experience.

![](http://jerseyrollz.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/BBQ-933x550.jpg)

## Jersey Rollz will cater any size event

From weddings, corporate lunches, private parties, and much more.  You can expect high quality food, great attention to detail, and excellent customer service – custom tailored for your event.  Our unique features include the ability to handle both large and small events, a completely self-contained food truck with a fully-equipped kitchen, and the ability and willingness to travel far for our clients!

## Catering Choices for Everyone

In addition to our gourmet eggrolls, we also offer other menus such as wings, cheese steaks, gourmet grilled cheese sandwiches, a taco bar and tater tots topped with the classic favorites – cheese, bacon or chili.
