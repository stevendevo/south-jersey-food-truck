About Angry Archie's

## One obsession with bold seafood.

In 2015, Chef Chris Curado launched Angry Archie's with a single food truck and a simple conviction: Jersey City deserved seafood that didn't cut corners. A decade later, that truck has become three — plus a brick-and-mortar restaurant at 565 Palisade Ave. The standards haven't changed.

[Our Story](https://angryarchies.com/about.html)

**Seafood done differently** From lobster rolls to loaded fries — bold flavor, real ingredients, no shortcuts.

**Restaurant & food truck** Two ways to get Archie's food, with each experience built to stand on its own.

**Jersey City original** 565 Palisade Ave is home base. The truck takes the show on the road.
