### The story behind our food — why event planners choose AhPizza Food Truck

At AhPizza Food Truck, great pizza starts with quality ingredients, authentic recipes, and a commitment to creating memorable experiences. Based in Flemington, New Jersey, our team serves fresh handcrafted pizzas and Italian-inspired favorites at events throughout the tri-state area. Whether we're catering a corporate lunch, wedding reception, street fair, brewery event, school fundraiser, or private party, our goal is simple: deliver amazing food with professional service that guests remember. We take pride in serving hot, fresh pizza directly from our mobile kitchen while creating a fun and interactive dining experience for every event.

## Location

Conveniently based in Flemington, New Jersey, AhPizza Food Truck proudly serves communities throughout Hunterdon County and the surrounding tri-state region. Our mobile catering service travels across New Jersey, Eastern Pennsylvania, and the New York metropolitan area for festivals, corporate functions, weddings, private parties, school events, sporting events, and community celebrations. No matter where your event takes place, our team delivers fresh food, professional service, and a memorable food truck experience your guests will love.

+

AhPizza Food Truck is known for fresh handcrafted pizza, professional event catering, and bringing an authentic pizza-shop experience directly to festivals, weddings, corporate events, private parties, and community gatherings throughout the tri-state area.

+

Our menu features handcrafted pizzas, specialty pizzas, Italian favorites, appetizers, and event-friendly menu options designed to serve groups of all sizes. Menu selections may vary depending on the event package.
