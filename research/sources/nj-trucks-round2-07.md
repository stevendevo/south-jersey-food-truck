Truck Catering

## The full Archie's experience, at your event.

Booking the food truck means we bring everything — the truck, the gear, the crew — and serve your guests fresh food on-site. It's the most memorable way to have Archie's at your event, and it works at any scale.

We work with corporate clients, private hosts, festival organizers, and brand activation teams.

### Truck catering is great for

Birthday parties & backyard eventsOffice lunches & employee appreciationFestivals, markets & pop-upsBranded activations & corporate eventsWeddings & milestone celebrations

Typical truck bookings range from $1,500–$5,000 depending on headcount and service time. Popular dates fill fast — we recommend booking 3–4 weeks ahead.
