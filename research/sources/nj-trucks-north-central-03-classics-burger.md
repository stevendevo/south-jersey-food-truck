# Private Events

# Your Event, Flawlessly Executed

Whether it’s a backyard birthday bash, corporate luncheon, wedding afterparty, or street festival, _Classics_ makes it easy to bring the smash burger experience to any event — thanks to the coordination skills of **Dora Kyriakakis**, the truck’s dedicated event manager.

With a passion for creating unforgettable experiences, Dora oversees all private bookings, catering events, and special activities. From the initial inquiry to the final burger served, she ensures each event runs smoothly and exceeds expectations.

**“Every event is personal to me,”** says Dora. “Whether it’s a wedding or a team lunch, my goal is to make it stress-free and memorable. When people trust us with their special day, I treat it like my own.”

Dora works closely with each client to tailor the menu, timing, and logistics, ensuring the food truck adds a flavorful and seamless touch to any celebration.

Let us roll up and make your event a hit— **contact our event coordinator, Dora Kyriakakis, today to book Classics Food Truck for your private event!**
