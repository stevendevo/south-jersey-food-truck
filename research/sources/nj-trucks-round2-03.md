## About Us

Mobile Cuisine and Catering

Fork in the Road brings you made-to-order food ranging from classic American favorites, like burgers, fries, and grilled cheese to specialized gourmet menus with ethnic flair, quality ingredients, and refined technique for cocktail parties, weddings, and corporate events.

We will serve you and your guests from our big, red food truck and trailer with restaurant quality straight to your front door throughout New Jersey homes and venues.

Contact us for your next party or special event.
