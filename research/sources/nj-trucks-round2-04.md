## WHO   ARE   WE?

# BACKYARD PARTIES   PRIVATE EVENTS   CATERING   POP-UPS   & ANYWHERE ON   THE STREETS OF NJ

Meet Joe & Ben, founders of NJ Ghostburger!

Two Jersey natives with a passion for quality food and bringing people together who decided to take their combined 40 years of experience in the food industry and follow their dream! Joe "The Meat Man" & Ben "Benny Foods" met in 2016 and realized that one's lifelong work in the Meat industry and the other's in Food Distribution was the perfect match to open up a burger truck!

​

It was clear from the start that what mattered most to both of them was top quality, locally sourced ingredients, 100% beef burgers, hand cut fries, and all of their NJ favorites made with love... and with their mutual passion for entertaining & hospitality, it was a recipe for success!
